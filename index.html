Being online isn't the same as being self-reliant online.

DigiLaya is a guided, community-based Digital Checkup that helps residents of Cabuyao secure their accounts, organize their files, and spot scams — using the phones they already carry, taught by neighbors trained to keep teaching it.

Connectivity isn't the gap. Confidence is.

Most residents already use their phones daily for messaging, transactions, and government services. What's often missing is repeatable practice — knowing how to recover a locked account, tell a real message from a scam, or keep important files somewhere they can actually find again. DigiLaya teaches these as habits, not lectures, and participants never hand over a password, OTP, or recovery code to anyone.

Identify each participant's digital needs and weak spots before anything else.

Walk through basic device and account security, on the participant's own phone.

Bring order to important files, photos, and documents.

Build the habit of telling legitimate messages and links apart from scams.

Set up practical account-recovery and emergency-access routines.

Participants create a simple 30-day plan to help them maintain the digital practices they learned beyond the session.

Built to outlast the three months it runs.

Every session trains the people who will run the next one.

Three months, three phases.

Validate and prepare

Month 1

Confirm partners and sites, run a baseline needs assessment, localize the learning materials, and recruit the first Community Guides.

Implement and monitor

Month 2

Train facilitators, run the guided Digital Checkup sessions in each barangay, and refine delivery based on what participants need.

Transfer and prove continuity

Month 3

Check which habits stuck, let trained Guides lead a session with light support, and hand over the Implementation Manual.
